DQ9 Rust — tech demo: help and limits

Select your own European Dragon Quest IX ROM (YDQP01), then click "New game". A DS save can also be loaded. These files stay in your browser: they are never sent to the server.

The keyboard is active as soon as the ROM is read: arrows to walk, Enter or Space to talk and advance messages, Q/E to turn the camera. In battle, arrows select, Enter confirms, and X, Backspace or Esc cancels. Items uses the acting character's carried items, not the bag. M mutes the sound; the browser only allows it after an interaction. Clicking the image takes the keyboard back at any time, and changing a setting returns it to the game.

Experimental version: animated roaming monsters now appear on the field and contact starts a played battle with command menus and messages. Standalone battle 26 has been checked to victory in the browser; the continuous story route is not certified. Field menus and shops are still missing; effects and battle motions are incomplete. Recent browser with WebGPU or WebGL 2 required; computer and keyboard recommended. No ROM is provided.
