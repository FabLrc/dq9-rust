DQ9 Rust — tech demo: help and limits

Select your own European Dragon Quest IX ROM (YDQP01), then click "New game". A DS save can also be loaded. These files stay in your browser: they are never sent to the server.

The keyboard is active as soon as the ROM is read: arrows to walk, Enter or Space to talk and advance messages, Q/E to turn the camera. I opens the field menu (Items, Equipment, Attributes, Spells); in battle and in a menu, arrows select, Enter confirms, and X, Backspace or Esc cancels. In battle, Items uses the acting character's carried items, not the bag. M mutes the sound; the browser only allows it after an interaction. Clicking the image takes the keyboard back at any time, and changing a setting returns it to the game.

Experimental version: animated roaming monsters now appear on the field and contact starts a played battle with command menus and messages. Browser checks cover natural Hexagon contact, cancellation, flight and a defeat returning to the church; standalone battle 26 was won with ROM-backed attack/reaction/death/flight motions, and the field menu, items, equipment, spells and the inn/shop/church screens have been driven on the canvas (one copy per trade). The continuous story route is not certified; the field's chests and "examine", battle camera/effects and companions are incomplete. Recent browser with WebGPU or WebGL 2 required; computer and keyboard recommended. No ROM is provided.

Graphics: "Enhanced" is the default layout (also ?layout=enhanced): a single widescreen field with mipmapped textures, bloom, screen-space contact shadows, filmic tonemapping, colour grading, a vignette, dithering, the single-pixel edge accent and a 16:9 interface showing the whole party. Every effect has its own checkbox above (or ?fx= / ?nofx=, e.g. ?nofx=bloom,vignette); with every effect off the field keeps the DS look. "Enhanced (reactive, experimental)" (?layout=enhanced-reactive) fills any window, portrait to ultra-wide, without bars. "stacked", "side" and "top" keep the faithful DS presentation.

Field screens: the original draws the field's 3D view and its message window on the bottom (touch) screen and its map screen on the top one; this port draws them on the top screen by default. "3D at the bottom" (or ?screens=original) is the original's assignment; the top screen is then a plain placeholder until the map screen is read, and a battle is unchanged. Both work with every screen layout.
